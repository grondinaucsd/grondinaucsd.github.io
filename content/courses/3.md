## Introduction

The goal in this class is to learn how the macroeconomy works. We approach the subject as social scientists following the [scientific method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_method): explore the data to ask questions, develop hypotheses/explanations grounded in the behavior of the actors involved (households, workers, businesses, banks, the government), and critically evaluate how well those hypotheses match what we observe in reality.

## Module Yellow: Measuring the Economy: Income, Prices, and Labor

Module Yellow introduces how we measure income, prices, and labor to describe the state of the macroeconomy.

---

## Module Red: Employment, Prices, and Income Distribution

Module Red examines wage setting, price setting, the aggregate labor/product model, and income inequality to explain employment, prices, and income distribution.

---

## Module Green: The Financial System

Module Green explores borrowing and lending, banks, money, and financial instruments to explain how the financial system works.

---

## Module Blue: Unemployment, Inflation, and Macroeconomic Policy

Module Blue studies business cycles, the multiplier model, inflation, and monetary policy to understand macroeconomic fluctuations and policy responses.

---

## Module Purple: Economic Growth

Module Purple examines the Malthusian trap, technology and innovation, and the present and future of economic growth.
