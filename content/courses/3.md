## Introduction

The goal in this class is to learn how the macroeconomy works. We approach the subject as social scientists following the [scientific method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_method): explore the data to ask questions, develop hypotheses/explanations grounded in the behavior of the actors involved (households, workers, businesses, banks, the government), and critically evaluate how well those hypotheses match what we observe in reality.

+ [Syllabus](/static/Econ_3_Syllabus.pdf)

The suggested readings below are from CORE Econ’s open-access [The Economy 2.0: Macroeconomics](https://books.core-econ.org/the-economy/macroeconomics/0-3-contents.html).
